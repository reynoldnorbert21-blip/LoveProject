# Red Candle — Episode 1: Green Candles

Style: **Impasto Oil Painting** (palette-knife paint, drawn entirely in code).
Format: vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, about 2:13. Malayalam dialogue (ElevenLabs), Malayalam + English subtitles.
Source: "Red Candle — Malayalam Short Film Script" by the user, Episode 1, shots 1–14.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Straight chronology.** Airport → Manchester → job → trading → resignation. Clear, but it gives away the hook: we never feel the danger.
2. **Bookend with the cold open (the script's own).** 3 a.m. "Payout: Processing" → title → four years earlier → the climb → the red candle. The hook in the first 3 s is the payout screen; the ending answers it with the opposite colour.
3. **Two colours as the spine.** Same story, but colour itself tells it: the world is painted warm and cool, and **red appears only on screens** — a red pixel in the opening, the title candle, and the final giant red candle that floods the room. Green grows as he wins; red is held back until the last shot.

**Chosen: 2 + 3.** The user's script already has the bookend; impasto lets colour be an event ("the one coloured thing"), so the red candle becomes the film's native move.

## 2. Logline and arc

A Kerala boy in Manchester claws from dish-washing to a calm IT job, finds trading through a friend, wins early and quits — while the friend who pushed him keeps his own job safe.

Setup (airport, cold, dishes, fear) → Turn (Jithin's phone in the pub; the first green numbers) → Ending (he walks out a winner; one red candle drops).

## 3. Benchmark

- *Learn from*: slow-burn character dramas told in vertical shorts (one idea per shot, faces large in frame, text kept for screens); palette-knife night-street painting for warm lamps against cold blue rain.
- *Don't take*: any real broker, app or brand UI; any existing film's shots, characters or music.
