# RED CANDLE — a MeowXTrader series
Three episodes of about 3 minutes each · vertical 9:16 · Malayalam dialogue, English subtitles · Impasto oil-painting style

**What the series is about:** a boy carries his family's dream across the world, loses everything to a market he didn't respect and a friend he trusted, and comes home only after he learns the one rule that matters.

**Emotional spine:** one question, asked three times. In each episode Amma asks "Did you eat?" (കഴിച്ചോ?). The answer changes:
- **Episode 1:** a smiling lie.
- **Episode 2:** a lie through tears.
- **Episode 3:** the truth: "I came home to eat your food."

**Second thread:** Achan's watch. In Episode 1 he says "don't waste time". In Episode 3 Nikhil answers "I did waste it, Achan. But I won it back."

**Music:** an original score, no film songs.
- **Family theme:** a harmonium-and-flute melody that returns whenever home is near.
- **Episode 2:** the same theme goes sour on a lone violin.
- **Episode 3:** the theme returns as a full comeback BGM with chenda-style drums and big strings.
